A digital business card and a website may both contain your name, contact details and links. They still have different jobs.

The card helps a person you have already met or reached connect with you quickly. The website helps a wider audience understand, compare and trust the business.

<div class="tldr" data-speakable>
**Use a digital business card for quick personal sharing and contact actions. Use a website for deeper information, search visibility, services, proof and conversion journeys. Many professionals benefit from both, with the card acting as a simple doorway to the website.**
</div>

## What a digital business card does well

A digital card is designed for a short mobile visit. It can bring together:

- Name, role and organisation
- Phone, email and messaging actions
- A save-contact option
- Website and selected social links
- A QR code for in-person sharing
- A short professional introduction

It is useful at meetings, exhibitions, networking events and in WhatsApp conversations. Unlike a printed card, approved profile details can be updated without reprinting.

## What a website does better

A website can explain several services, publish useful articles, show projects, answer detailed questions and support forms, ecommerce or customer portals.

It can also create search landing pages for people who do not already know your name. A digital card is usually too small to carry that whole responsibility.

## When a digital card may be enough

An individual consultant, representative or early-stage professional may initially need only a clear identity and direct contact options. A card can provide that compact presence.

Before relying on it alone, ask whether customers need to compare services, see substantial proof, find the business through Google or complete a longer enquiry journey.

## When to use both

Use the digital card as the personal layer and the website as the business layer.

For example, a sales representative can share a card with direct contact actions, while linking to a product page, case example or booking page on the main website. This keeps the card focused without hiding the information needed for a larger decision.

## Keep the profile useful

Do not add every social account simply because space is available. Prioritise the actions a real contact will take. Check the profile on a phone and keep information current.
