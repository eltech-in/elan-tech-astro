15 August represents courage, independence, and the confidence to build a better future. For eLan Technology, the date also holds a deeply personal meaning.

On 15 August 2026, we celebrate 21 years since the official launch of eLan Technology.

Our journey began earlier. In 2002, eLan started as a freelance and startup unit driven by curiosity, creativity, and the belief that technology could help businesses discover new possibilities. Those formative years gave us the experience and purpose that led to the official launch of eLan Technology on 15 August 2005.

</div>

## From a small beginning to a digital technology company

The web was a very different place in 2002. Websites were simpler, mobile commerce had not arrived, and artificial intelligence belonged more to research laboratories than everyday business.

What has remained constant is our approach: understand the real business problem, design with purpose, and build technology that creates lasting value.

Over the years, we have worked across website design, custom web development, ecommerce, digital marketing, business portals, accessibility, and emerging web technologies. Every project has contributed to our growth - not only in technical capability, but also in how we listen, collaborate, and take responsibility for outcomes.

## Independence through technology

The idea of independence has always resonated with our work.

For a growing business, digital independence can mean owning a professional online presence, reaching customers beyond geographical boundaries, reducing dependence on manual processes, or choosing technology that can evolve with the organisation.

Today, we help businesses create that independence through fast, accessible websites and custom digital platforms.

Our recent ecommerce work reflects this direction. We designed and developed a [custom Shopify storefront for Rangresha](/portfolio/case-study/rangresha/), creating a tailored shopping experience for its sustainable handcrafted products.

For [Puritas](/portfolio/case-study/puritas/), we built a custom Medusa.js ecommerce platform with subscription commerce, Razorpay and PhonePe payment modules, and purpose-built shipping workflows.

These projects represent where eLan Technology is heading: combining thoughtful design, modern engineering, and custom ecommerce development to solve increasingly sophisticated business challenges.

## Entering the age of AI and agentic commerce

The next chapter of the web is already taking shape.

Artificial intelligence is changing how websites are designed, developed, searched, and experienced. Ecommerce is moving toward agentic commerce, where AI systems can help customers discover, compare, and purchase products.

Our responsibility is not simply to follow a trend. It is to understand which technologies create genuine value and apply them responsibly.

As we move forward, eLan Technology will place greater focus on [custom Shopify development](/services/ecommerce/shopify-development/), [Medusa.js commerce platforms](/services/ecommerce/medusa-js-development/), AI-ready websites, accessible digital experiences, and high-performance web engineering.

## Thank you for being part of the journey

No organisation builds a 21-year history alone.

We are grateful to every client who trusted us with an idea, every team member who contributed their talent, every partner who supported our work, and every well-wisher who encouraged us along the way.

From our beginnings as a freelance and startup unit in 2002 to the official launch of eLan Technology on 15 August 2005 - and now to a future shaped by ecommerce and AI - this has been a journey of continuous learning and reinvention.

On India’s Independence Day, we celebrate the freedom to imagine, create, and build.

**Happy Independence Day.**

**And happy 21st anniversary to eLan Technology.**
