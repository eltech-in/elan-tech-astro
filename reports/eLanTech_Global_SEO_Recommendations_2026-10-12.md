# eLan Technology: SEO, International Visibility and Enquiry Growth

Prepared: 12 October 2026. Reporting period: 11 July–10 October 2026 (92 days).

This is an independent assessment of the supplied GSC, GA4, PageSpeed and SEOptimer exports, supplemented by selected live-site checks and current official guidance. Recommendations are not ranking guarantees. No website, account or external listing was changed while preparing this report.

## 1. Executive assessment

The immediate problem is declining search exposure, weak non-branded click acquisition and an unreliable enquiry-measurement baseline—not a generally slow or uncrawlable website.

The site received **132 Google clicks from 14,786 impressions**, a **0.89% CTR**, during the 92-day period. The most recent 28 days produced **28 clicks versus 40** in the preceding 28 days. Impressions fell **31.8%**, while CTR remained almost unchanged. Average position improved within the remaining impression mix; that does not mean every important keyword improved.

My recommended international programme has three lanes:

1. **Capture existing demand: US and Canada.** The US already supplies substantial impressions. Canada combines some visibility with relevant project and testimonial evidence.
2. **Build a focused commercial pilot: UAE.** Treat it as a strategic expansion market, not an already-proven high-volume SEO market. Use ecommerce, business systems and white-label development positioning rather than a generic “best Dubai agency” claim.
3. **Develop selectively: Australia, then New Zealand; Japan after localisation readiness.** Retain separate country pages, but do not invest equally in every market immediately.

Keep India as the operating and lead-generation base. It supplies **92 of 132 clicks**, approximately 70%. International expansion should add to this base, not erase Nagpur relevance or restart homepage headline experiments.

### Important limitations

- The two landing-page CSVs are byte-identical: they are counted once.
- The query export contains 686 rows but only **59 clicks and 6,418 impressions**. It is not the complete query universe. Branded/non-branded proportions calculated from it must not be presented as whole-site proportions.
- The page export sums to 133 clicks and 16,160 impressions, unlike the chart's 132 and 14,786. Dimension-level exports can differ from property totals. Use the chart for site totals; use page rows for prioritisation.
- A country's search totals represent searchers in that country across the site. A country landing page's totals represent that URL across all countries. These are different populations.
- There is no country × query × page comparison export here. We cannot yet identify exactly which US queries and URLs caused its low CTR, or which markets drove the recent decline.
- GA4 first-user acquisition and session acquisition answer different questions; they should not be added together.
- A small sample, an average position or a long question-shaped query does not prove an AI citation, a buying enquiry or a stable first-page ranking.
- These files do not include a fresh GSC indexing-reason export or full server access logs. They cannot establish that a penalty, robots block or 404 problem caused the impression decline.

Append the brand where practical, assess rendered width and avoid introducing a local-office implication. Google can rewrite titles and snippets; changing a meta description is not a guaranteed CTR gain.

## 6. Technical findings: what deserves attention

### Already working in selected live checks

- `/sitemap-index.xml` returns HTTP 200 XML and points to `/sitemap-0.xml`.
- The child sitemap returns HTTP 200 and contains **101 URLs**. No manifest, VCF or image URLs were found among its `<loc>` entries.
- The supplied `urllist.txt` includes a manifest URL, but it is not evidence that this manifest is in the current XML sitemap.
- `/robots.txt` allows ordinary Google crawling and declares the sitemap.
- US, Canada and UAE country pages returned HTTP 200 with self-canonicals.
- The expired `/pricing/digital-launchpad/` URL redirects **301 to `/pricing/`**, which returns 200. Retirement is already implemented.
- Live `llms.txt` includes the correct identity history, contact details, conditional ownership terms and Zoho scope.

These are spot checks, not a complete crawl or authenticated GSC URL Inspection. Sitemap availability does not prove every URL is indexed.

### Prioritised improvements

**Accessibility and mobile usability — high priority.** The supplied PageSpeed run scores accessibility 94 and specifically flags the homepage's amber “Flagship ecommerce practice” label, emerald Shopify labels/links, cyan Medusa labels/links and red Zoho labels/links for contrast. Adjust colours by theme and test actual contrast. Footer “Cancellation & Refund” and “Privacy & cookie settings” controls need larger effective targets or appropriate spacing. WCAG 2.2's minimum target criterion is 24×24 CSS pixels with exceptions, not a universal demand for one particular gap. [W3C target-size guidance](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/target-size-minimum.html).

**First-visit consent layout — high priority for conversion.** The PageSpeed screenshot shows the consent panel taking substantial mobile space above the contact bar. Test small screens, zoom, keyboard access, dismissal and reopening. Keep refusal and withdrawal usable; do not turn analytics on automatically to make reports look healthier.

**404 diagnosis — high priority.** Two “Page Not Found” title variants recorded 48 and four views: **52 views in total**, not 52 visitors or confirmed lost enquiries. Obtain exact requested paths, referrers and server status codes. Fix internal broken links; use relevant redirects only where a genuine replacement exists. Do not redirect every missing path to the homepage.

**Historical case-study URLs — targeted review.** The page export contains 38 `/portfolio/case-study/` rows, but the current sitemap contains none. They can be historical remnants of deliberately withheld case studies. Check their current destination and internal links individually. Do not republish unsupported case studies merely to remove an indexing warning.

**Robots rule consistency — medium priority.** Several named-crawler groups independently allow `/`, whereas admin/API restrictions appear only in the wildcard group. Specific groups do not automatically inherit wildcard restrictions under the documented Google grouping model. Review this structure and ensure intended restrictions are consistent for supported crawlers. Robots rules are not access control: private files and lead records need server-side protection. This is a configuration hygiene finding, not an established cause of falling Google clicks. [Robots grouping guidance](https://developers.google.com/crawling/docs/robots-txt/robots-txt-spec).

### Speed: optimise the evidence, not a grade

The supplied mobile PageSpeed run reports **97 performance, 94 accessibility, 100 best practices and 100 SEO**, with LCP 1.4 seconds, FCP 1.2 seconds, TBT 60 ms and CLS 0.096. SEOptimer reports mobile 89 and desktop 90, including different load/speed-index measurements. Different test environments and timings prevent treating them as a single result.

There is insufficient field data in the audit for a reliable real-user Core Web Vitals assessment. A 97 lab score does not prove a field pass; TBT is not INP.

Recommended engineering work:

- Repeat tests on the homepage, priority country page, ecommerce page, audit form and digital card, including before/after consent.
- Measure real response time from target regions before changing hosts. Check HTTP/www/canonical redirect chains; retain one-hop canonical redirects where feasible.
- Investigate the roughly 30 KiB of unused client JavaScript and the reported unattributed forced reflow. Do not blame a specific component without profiling.
- Use responsive image sizes for the hero and logos. The PDF estimates around 44 KiB of potential image savings—not a reason to degrade all assets. Preserve high-density displays, legibility and uncropped logo proportions.
- Investigate layout shifts near the 0.1 threshold, including fonts, image space, consent and delayed UI.
- Do not introduce blanket CDN caching for form endpoints, personalised responses or consent-dependent content.

### Audit recommendations not worth implementing blindly

Do not add Meta Pixel just to satisfy SEOptimer when there is no advertising programme or consent-approved use. Do not replace readable email addresses with images. Do not chase arbitrary word counts, YouTube subscriber counts or wholesale removal of intentional inline styling as SEO fixes.

The PageSpeed agentic audit flags llms.txt as having no links. The live file does contain plain URL references; this looks like a formatting/parser issue, not a missing file. Converting resource entries into explicit Markdown links is a sensible low-priority cleanup. It is not a ranking fix: Google says special AI files or schema are not required for its AI search features. [Google AI-search guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/ai-features).
