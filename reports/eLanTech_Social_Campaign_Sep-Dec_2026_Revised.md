## 2. What changed after checking the supplied documents

The source plans contain useful production ideas, but some claims should not be carried into decisions or promotional copy.

| Source statement | Correction and effect on this plan |
|---|---|
| One form submission proves only one enquiry and 93% abandonment | These are event counts, not a verified user funnel. Fourteen form-start events and one form-submit event cannot establish unique users, abandonment or delivered leads. Test forms and tracking before spending; do not announce that the website is broken. |
| 1,274 active users | The supplied retention export's Web platform row reports 1,121 active users. Do not sum daily or weekly active users into a period total. |
| AI assistants represent about 10% of all sessions | The channel table sums to 1,600 sessions. AI Assistant is 114, approximately 7.1%. ChatGPT is 94 sessions in the source table. Attribution is evidence of referral visits, not proof of which article was cited. |
| 82% of all search clicks are branded | The report calculates this from 67 visible query clicks, not the property's 162 clicks. Query exports omit some data. Brand concentration is worth examining, but do not apply the percentage to all traffic. |
| Average position 17.6 | Weighting the supplied daily positions by impressions gives approximately 17.13. Positions are blended metrics, not fixed page numbers. |
| AI Overviews caused the GEO article's zero clicks | Plausible hypothesis, not established. Investigate page-filtered queries, countries and search presentation before deciding. |
| Title changes reset Google's understanding | Not established by these exports. Titles should be stable enough to evaluate, but useful corrections need not wait 90 days. GA4 title rows alone do not prove they all belong to one URL. |
| Portfolio is dead | Zero search clicks is not zero business value. Portfolio views can support referral/social conversions. Previous case-study removals were intentional; do not recreate unsupported case studies. |
| Claims of 48 followers, 11 reviews and competitor review counts | Treat as unverified account baselines. Capture current account Insights before setting numerical growth targets. |
| GBP posts expire in seven days; enable the old messaging feature | Current Google guidance says posts older than six months are archived unless a date range is set. Legacy native chat ended in 2024; check whether this profile currently offers eligible WhatsApp/text contact options. |
| A cross-domain canonical means “do not index” | Canonicals are preference signals, not a noindex directive. Verify production canonical, redirects, sitemap and share previews together. |
| Product testimonials are definitely fake | Their authenticity has not been established here. Remove from promotion unless substantiated and approved; do not accuse named people of fabrication. |
| Free lifetime beta access in exchange for testimonials | No lifetime promise without commercial approval. Pilot access and feedback must not depend on a positive public review. Ask separately for permission to quote honest feedback and disclose incentives where relevant. |
| Broadcast to the full list / share every post to a group | Segment by relevance and permission, honour opt-outs and obtain group-admin approval. A saved contact is not blanket consent. |
| 200 product footer links create an SEO advantage | Use optional, relevant attribution for discovery, not as a manufactured backlink strategy. Referral counts do not identify the exact link placement clicked. |
| Fixed word counts or FAQ schema guarantee improvement | No word-count target or rich-result promise. Publish useful buyer answers supported by evidence. |

### Visual identity

- Bright cream/white bases; eLan blue and orange. Shopify green, Medusa indigo, WooCommerce purple, accessibility teal as accents.
- Product identity follows final branding, not an assumed product name or inherited draft logo.
- Real screenshots and actual people before generic AI imagery. No generated client portrait, fabricated review screenshot or synthetic product screen presented as real.
- Logo modestly sized. One headline, one message, one action. No overcrowded feature lists or tiny tax disclaimers.
- Caption spoken video; descriptive alt text where supported; sufficient contrast; no fast flashing; avoid music overpowering speech.
- A button drawn on artwork is not clickable. Pair it with a real platform action/link; on Instagram feed, use a clear profile-link or relevant DM path.
- QR codes work better on a second screen or print. Always include a short written URL or tappable link as an alternative for mobile viewers.
- Use English for LinkedIn and international posts. Test Hindi/Marathi or Hinglish voiceovers for relevant local audiences with English captions; do not mix every language onto one graphic.

### Caption structures

Proof: client requirement → actual work delivered → screenshot/demo → one next step.

Education: buyer question → two or three useful answers → limitation where needed → relevant service/guide.

Offer: audience → confirmed inclusions → price/tax/expiry → terms → action.

Product: real use situation → working feature → plan availability → demo/signup link.

## 10. Measurement, tests and budget rules

### Capture baselines on 11 September

Record account followers, reach, profile visits, link clicks, GBP actions/reviews, Pinterest outbound clicks, WhatsApp conversations, verified successful form submissions and proposals. Current account counts require account access or exported Insights.

### Two separate funnels

Agency: social exposure → relevant visit/conversation → qualified enquiry → proposal → won project.

Product: campaign visit → signup → card published → first share → paid subscription → renewal/cancellation.

Do not combine low-value card signups and high-value agency leads into one “conversion” total. Separate paid, organic, product and agency results.

### Link tracking

Example: `?utm_source=linkedin&utm_medium=social&utm_campaign=q4_2026_commerce&utm_content=oct06_puritas_demo`.

Use platform-specific sources and a consistent medium that is tested against GA4 channel rules. Use `paid_social` for paid social. WhatsApp and GBP may need a documented custom channel grouping; do not assume every tagged visit becomes Organic Social.

Never put names, emails or phone numbers in UTM parameters. Preserve attribution through forms and product signup where feasible. For direct WhatsApp links, use a harmless campaign code in the prefilled message and record it in the enquiry log; a click is not a conversation.

Suggested events, only after implementation/testing: `generate_lead` on accepted agency submissions; separate phone/WhatsApp click events; product signup, card publication and paid-subscription confirmation. Do not send sensitive/personal fields to analytics. Reconcile server/payment records before treating events as sales.

### Weekly review

Record post ID, channel, published URL, format, hook, audience, reach, meaningful engagement, outbound visits, qualified enquiries, proposals, subscriptions and production time. Link a person only in an appropriately secured CRM, not a public report.

Test one variable at a time across comparable posts: first-frame headline, demo versus carousel, or CTA. At low volumes, avoid declaring a winner from one enquiry or a few clicks. Consider quality and production effort together.

Initial posting-time experiments: LinkedIn 9–11 am IST; FB/IG lunchtime versus early evening; WhatsApp during recipients' business hours. These are hypotheses, not universal best times. Adjust to account Insights and international audience time zones.

### Monthly reviews

- 26 September: Launchpad closure, attributed enquiries, proposals/bookings and alert reliability.
- 30 September: initial account baselines and production capacity.
- 30 October: city/service audience response and digital-card pilot readiness.
- 30 November: product activation/subscriptions, agency leads and content formats worth repeating.
- 31 December: campaign summary; renewal observations only for cohorts that have had time to renew; January priorities.

### Targets and paid activity

Use operational targets first: all published links tested; all enquiries assigned; two useful posts weekly after the offer; every public claim approved; no unhandled delivery failures. Set commercial targets after the first month of reliable attribution and a capacity/budget discussion.

No guaranteed 8–20 leads, 250 followers or 150 signups. The available exports cannot support those forecasts.

Paid tests require separate approval. Start only after conversion/notification tests pass. Compare one real demo with one benefit-led creative; define total spend cap and a review point before starting. Evaluate cost per qualified enquiry or paid subscriber, not only cost per click. Keep Launchpad, custom commerce and subscription audiences/budgets separate. Avoid paid distribution of accessibility fear claims.
